## Why we believe it
The initial 44-page corpus cleanly separates verified primary concepts from draft notes using 0.75 and 0.45 cutoffs.

## How we validate it
Run claim-level verification audits across 100+ ingested pages and measure whether pages scored above 0.75 contain zero unsupported factual claims.
